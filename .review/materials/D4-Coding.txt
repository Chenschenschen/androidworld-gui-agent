FORMALIZATION
Correct Execution ≠ Correct ModelingReal decisionReposition vehicles after a transit disruption.What should improve?
Interpret / formalizeChoose variables, objective, constraints and assumptions.
ExecuteRun the optimizer exactly.Return a feasible plan.
Inspect resultFeasible ≠ useful.Optimal ≠ appropriate.Check the real goal.
Same solver, different formulationWrong model: minimize relocation distance → cheap movement, but long waiting time in the disrupted areaBetter model: wait time + cost + coverage risk, with capacity, labor and approval constraints
Modeling errors hide in:Objectivewrong outcomeConstraintmissing ruleAssumptionbad forecastBoundaryneeds approval
